Meet your coach

Michael Zinkann is a USA Cycling Level 3 Coach and Category 1 road racer based in Newnan, Georgia, specializing in road races, criteriums and gravel.

Known for his tactical sense, breakaway skill and aggressive racing style, Michael brings decades of elite-level race experience directly to his athletes. He currently races on the Domestic Elite team The Paceline Project p/b LC Food.

A proven winner at the highest levels of domestic racing, Michael’s career highlights include:

  • 1st – 2026 Georgia State Championship P1/2 Road Race
  • 2nd – 2023 USA Cycling Masters National Championship Criterium
  • 1st – Georgia State Road Race & Criterium Championships (Masters)
  • Extensive experience in top-tier U.S. criterium blocks, including Athens Twilight, Speed Week, the USA Crits series, Gateway Cup and Armed Forces

Professionally, Michael is an IT leader and Assistant Director for Coweta County Government, and he brings the same rigorous, data-driven approach to training. He uses TrainingPeaks and WKO5 to build precision training structures.

Michael is also a cycling and health advocate who serves on boards including Bike Coweta and Friends of the Linc.

Whether you want to master pack dynamics, rack up Strava KOMs, stand on the top step or simply improve your overall fitness, Michael provides the technical insight, motivation and individualized programming to bridge the gap between where you are now and your goals.
